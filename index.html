How to Play

Controls

  • ←↑→↓ steer the dog directly (screen directions)
  • C Come bye – flank clockwise around the sheep
  • A Away – flank anticlockwise
  • W Walk up – move straight at the sheep
  • Space / L Lie down – stop
  • S Steady – toggle half speed   B Get out   T That'll do
  • + / - or mouse wheel: zoom   M mute   R restart   Esc menu

On a phone

  • Drag anywhere on the field to steer the dog with the stick
  • Tap the command buttons to whistle; pinch with two fingers to zoom
  • On the field list, tap once to see a field and again to start

Scoring (90 points)

Outrun 20 · Lift 10 · Fetch 20 · Drive 30 · Pen 10. Points come off for crossing the course, pushing the sheep into a panic, running off line, missing gates and breaks at the pen. The clock starts when you send the dog; the gate closes once all five sheep stand in the pen.

Practice drills

Each drill sets up one part of the course and judges it exactly as in a trial. When a run is complete the score and the costliest fault show briefly, then the sheep are set out again. Press R to reset early.
